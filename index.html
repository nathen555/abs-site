ABS is a sophisticated business club that teaches what business is about. It has multiple aspects and areas in business such as sales, management, tech, AI, finance, stocks, and how to handle money. We dive deep in many of these topics for youth who are interested and excel in that area.

We also have basic skills for people who haven’t been connected to money who want to start but are too afraid of exploring something new. Remember this: the first rule in business is to be brave — not reckless, but brave.
Every session is hands-on, project-driven, as we have consultants who are masters in different areas along with one-to-one teaching sessions for beginners about the basic rules of business. We have programs that use digital currency on a real-basis market for simulation and training for investment. We also have learning videos from large company owners who teach us about the world market as a whole. No boring slideshows! Just your full interest!
